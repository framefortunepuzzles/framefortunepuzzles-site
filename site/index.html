Three ways to play

Every book comes in three editions. Same puzzles, so choose whichever suits you.

Paperback

Large 8.5 by 11 inch pages with plenty of room to write. Sold on Amazon, and a good gift for the fan who has everything.

Write-on PDF

Write on it with your pen on a Kindle Scribe, iPad or reMarkable. Tap a button to jump to the solutions and back. It does not check your answers for you.

Playable game

Plays in any web browser, even offline. It checks your answers and keeps score. Progress saves on your device, so clearing your browser data clears it.
