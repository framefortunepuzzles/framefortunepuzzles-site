Privacy

This site is a simple brochure for our books. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no advertising trackers, and we do not set cookies.

The free game

The sample game remembers your progress in your own browser's storage, on your device only. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data clears it.

Links to shops

Our buy buttons take you to Amazon, Payhip and Etsy. Once you are on those sites, their own privacy policies apply. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases, so Amazon may use a cookie to recognise that you arrived from this site. It does not change the price you pay.
